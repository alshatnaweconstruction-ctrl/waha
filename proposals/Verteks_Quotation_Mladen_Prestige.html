Project Description

The project involves the construction of a new gypsum board bathroom within the existing room, together with layout modifications to accommodate the new space and a complete drainage connection to the existing external manhole. Works cover plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, tiling, and finishing through to final site cleaning.

Scope of Work

  • Site preparation and protection of existing surfaces
  • Removal and reinstallation of the sliding aluminium door
  • Concrete cutting for the new drainage installation
  • Wall chasing where required for plumbing and electrical routing
  • New drainage installation to the external manhole
  • Plumbing installation
  • Gypsum board partition for the new bathroom
  • Electrical installation
  • Waterproofing
  • Floor and wall tiling
  • Concrete reinstatement
  • Finishing works
  • Waste removal
  • Final site cleaning
