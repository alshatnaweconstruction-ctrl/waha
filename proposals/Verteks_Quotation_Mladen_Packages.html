Dear Mladen, thank you for the opportunity to quote for your renovation project. This quotation is based on our site inspection and the requirements we discussed together, and sets out three package options so you can choose the finish level that best suits your project and your budget.

Project Description

The project involves the architectural renovation of the existing room to create a separate bedroom and a newly constructed, fully-equipped bathroom, including a new drainage line connected to the external manhole.

Scope of Work

  • Removal and reinstallation of the sliding aluminum door
  • Concrete cutting for the new drainage line
  • Drainage installation to the external manhole
  • Wall chasing for plumbing
  • Gypsum board partition for the new bathroom
  • Plumbing installation
  • Electrical modifications
  • Waterproofing
  • Wall and floor tiling
  • Restoration of concrete and walls after installation works
  • Site cleaning and waste removal
